# No. 054 Stayed In

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
Overhead photograph, looking straight down, of an unmade double bed in soft morning daylight from a window: a rumpled plain white cotton duvet and one white pillow. Lying flat on the duvet is a large printed black and white street map sheet, about 60 cm wide, a little narrower than the pillow is long. The map shows a quiet residential street: one road running across the middle, a terraced row of small houses along its upper side with long back gardens behind them, houses along the lower side, and a narrow river along the bottom edge. Hundreds of small hand-inked dark blue dots, each about 2 mm across, cover the map in a loose cloud: sparse at the edges, with a few in the back gardens, a few on the far pavement and one or two in the river, growing dense toward the middle of the road directly in front of one house in the terrace, where they merge into a solid dark patch. That one house is outlined in pencil with a small pencil cross inside it, and only a few dots fall inside its outline. A black fine-liner pen lies on the duvet beside the map. At the top edge of the frame, a smartphone lies face down on a small plain wooden bedside table. Natural soft colour, crisp contemporary documentary photograph, not staged. Square format, 1:1. No people, no logos, no brand names, no street names or any other text on the map.
```

## Image 2

```
Close up photograph, looking straight down, of part of a printed black and white street map lying on a slightly creased white duvet in soft daylight. In the upper half, a terraced row of small houses is drawn in thin black lines; one house in the row is outlined in grey pencil with a small pencil cross in its centre, and only a few small scattered ink dots fall inside that outline. Directly in front of that house, across the pavement and the middle of the road, small round dark blue ink dots, each about 2 mm across, crowd together so densely that they merge into a solid dark patch, then thin out into separate dots toward the opposite pavement at the bottom of the frame. The paper has a faint fold crease and the dots show the slight unevenness of a fine-liner pen pressed by hand. Natural colour, sharp focus on the dark patch. Square format, 1:1. No people, no hands, no logos, no brand names, no street names or any other text.
```
