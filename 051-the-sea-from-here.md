# No. 051 The Sea From Here

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
Documentary colour photograph, crisp and contemporary. An empty underground concrete car park at night, lit by long fluorescent tube lights on a low ceiling, rows of grey concrete pillars receding into the distance, a few worn white painted lines on the floor, no cars. In the centre foreground, on the bare concrete floor, a large pale cream and pink spiral conch shell, about 25 cm long, lies on its side with its wide opening facing left. Beside it on the floor lies a small black handheld audio recorder, about 15 cm long, with two short microphones crossed in an X at its top end; the microphones rest just inside the mouth of the shell. Scale: the shell is clearly longer than the recorder, about one and a half times its length. Camera low, about 30 cm above the floor and a little to the side, so the shell and recorder are sharp and the car park stretches behind them, softly out of focus. Cool greenish fluorescent light, hard shadows under the shell. Square format, 1:1. No people, no cars, no water, no logos, no brand names, no signs, no numbers, no text.
```

## Image 2

```
Colour snapshot photograph, warm and slightly soft, at dusk in late summer. A flat, wide wheat field far inland, ripe golden wheat stretching to a low, level horizon under a pale orange and lilac sky, no water, hills or buildings anywhere in sight. In the right third of the frame, at the very edge of the field, stands a thick old square wooden fence post about waist high, grey and cracked with age, its flat top about 20 cm across. On that flat top rests a large pale cream and pink spiral conch shell, about 25 cm long, lying on its side with its opening facing left and its tip overhanging the edge of the post a little. Beside it on the post lies a small black handheld audio recorder, about 15 cm long, with two short microphones crossed in an X at its top end, the microphones resting just inside the mouth of the shell. Scale: the shell is about one and a half times the length of the recorder. Camera at the height of the post, about two metres away, the post and objects sharp, the field and sky soft behind. Square format, 1:1. No people, no animals, no sea, no lakes or rivers, no fence wire in front of the objects, no logos, no brand names, no text.
```

Checks: the same pale conch shell and the same small black recorder in both shots; the recorder's microphones just inside the shell's mouth; shot 1 an empty underground car park at night under fluorescent tubes, shell on its side on the floor; shot 2 a wheat field edge at dusk, both objects on a thick wooden fence post, no water in sight; recorder clearly shorter than the shell; objects rest on their surfaces, nothing floating or merged, no legible text on the recorder.
