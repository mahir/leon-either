# No. 020 The Middle Word

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
Crisp contemporary colour photograph of one long wooden shelf in a quiet public library, shot straight on at eye level in soft daylight from a window out of frame. About thirty worn hardback and paperback books of mixed heights, faded cloth and paper spines in muted greens, reds, greys and browns, stand upright filling the shelf edge to edge. From the top edge of every book, exactly one thin plain white paper slip sticks up, each slip at a different height and at a slightly different point along the book's depth, so the white tops make an uneven rising and falling line along the whole row, like a pulse. Every book has one slip, no book has two. Spines carry no readable titles, only blurred worn lettering. Shelf above and below just visible, slightly out of focus. Documentary, calm, not staged. Square format, 1:1. No people, no logos, no brand names, no readable text.
```

## Image 2

```
Overhead photograph, looking straight down, of an old library book lying open on a pale wooden reading table in even daylight. Off-white pages, dense small printed body text that is slightly soft and unreadable, except one short word in the exact middle of the right-hand page, the word THE, which is sharp and lightly circled once in grey pencil. A thin plain white paper slip lies along the book's centre gutter. A short pencil rests on the table beside the book. Natural paper texture, slight curl of the pages, faint shadow at the gutter. Quiet, exact, documentary. Square format, 1:1. No people, no hands, no logos, no brand names, no other readable text besides THE.
```
