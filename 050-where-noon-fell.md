Low winter sunlight, crisp shadows, pale blue sky above the wall. Two terracotta plant pots with bare soil stand at the foot of the wall on grey paving stones. Camera at standing eye height, about three metres back, facing the wall straight on, the whole figure eight and the nail clearly visible. Plain, believable, unstaged. Square format, 1:1.
