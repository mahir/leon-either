# No. 056 Came With the Frame

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
Crisp contemporary colour photograph, documentary and unstaged, taken at eye level from the bottom of a narrow carpeted staircase in an ordinary British terraced house, soft afternoon daylight coming from a small window at the top of the stairs. The whole wall alongside the stairs, from the bottom step to the landing, is densely covered in dozens of mismatched second-hand picture frames hung close together in a loose salon-style cluster that rises with the stairs: cheap gold, silver, white, black and pale wood frames, small and medium sizes (roughly 10x15 cm to 20x25 cm), a few slightly crooked. Every frame holds a glossy generic sample photograph of the kind sold inside new frames: smiling models, a couple laughing on a beach, a small boy in a bright yellow raincoat, a family of three generations around a dining table, a woman with a dog, a bride and groom. The colours in the photos are a little too bright and cheerful against the plain magnolia wall. Worn beige stair carpet, a simple white-painted banister on the right. No people in the house, only the faces inside the frames. Square format, 1:1. No logos, no brand names, no price stickers, no text anywhere, no real or recognisable people.
```

## Image 2

```
Crisp contemporary colour close up, documentary and unstaged, of a single small picture frame hanging on a plain magnolia-painted wall in soft daylight, shot straight on and filling most of the frame. The frame is cheap gold-coloured moulding, about 13x18 cm, slightly scuffed at one corner. Behind its glass is a glossy generic sample photograph of the kind sold inside new frames: a smiling young boy in a bright yellow hooded raincoat standing on a beach under a pale sky, overly bright and cheerful. A thin, even line of grey dust lies along the top edge of the frame and the glass carries a faint reflection of a window. Just visible at the edges are parts of neighbouring frames on the same wall, one silver and one pale wood, out of focus. Square format, 1:1. No hands, no other people outside the photograph, no logos, no brand names, no price stickers, no text anywhere.
```

Checks: every frame holds a glossy sample photo of smiling models (no blank frames, no family-snapshot look); frames mismatched and second-hand; frames small against the stair treads and handrail; the close-up small scuffed gold frame with the boy in the yellow raincoat could hang on that same magnolia wall; thin dust line along the top edge; faces and any hands in the photos look physically real; no text or logos.
