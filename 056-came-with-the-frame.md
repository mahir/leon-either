# No. 056 Came With the Frame

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
Crisp contemporary colour photograph, documentary and unstaged, taken at eye level from the bottom of a narrow carpeted staircase in an ordinary British terraced house, soft afternoon daylight coming from a small window at the top of the stairs. The whole wall on the left alongside the stairs, from the bottom step up to the landing, is densely covered in about sixty mismatched second-hand picture frames hung close together in a loose salon-style cluster that rises with the stairs: cheap gold, silver, white, black and pale wood frames, a few slightly crooked, some with chipped corners, faded gilt or scratched glass, none of them grand or ornate. Scale: every frame is small, photo-frame size from 10x15 cm up to 20x25 cm at most, so even the nearest frame is no wider than about a quarter of a stair tread (treads are about 85 cm wide), and dozens of frames fit in the height of one storey. Every frame holds a glossy generic sample photograph of the kind sold inside new frames: smiling models, a couple laughing on a beach, a small boy in a bright yellow raincoat, a family of three generations around a dining table, a woman with a dog, a bride and groom. The colours in the photos are a little too bright and cheerful against the plain magnolia wall. Worn beige stair carpet, a simple white-painted banister on the right. No people in the house, only the faces inside the frames. Square format, 1:1. No logos, no brand names, no price stickers, no text anywhere, no real or recognisable people.
```

## Image 2

```
Crisp contemporary colour close up, documentary and unstaged, of a single small picture frame hanging on a plain magnolia-painted wall in soft daylight, shot straight on and filling most of the frame. The frame hangs in portrait orientation and is cheap, worn gold-coloured moulding about 13x18 cm with a thin beaded inner edge, the gilt rubbed through and slightly chipped at one corner, clearly second-hand. Behind its glass is a glossy generic sample photograph of the kind sold inside new frames: a small smiling boy of about six with short light brown hair, standing full length and facing the camera in a bright yellow hooded raincoat with the hood down, dark trousers, against a soft out-of-focus background of green trees, overly bright and cheerful. Along the top edge of the frame lies a thin, flat film of fine grey household dust, barely raised, like a pencil line, not fluffy and not a thick strip. The glass carries a faint reflection of a window. Just visible at the edges are parts of neighbouring frames on the same wall, one silver and one pale wood, out of focus. Square format, 1:1. No hands, no other people outside the photograph, no logos, no brand names, no price stickers, no text anywhere.
```

Checks: every frame holds a glossy sample photo of smiling models (no blank frames, no family-snapshot look); frames mismatched, small and visibly second-hand; frames small against the stair treads and handrail; the close-up gold frame and the boy in the yellow raincoat match the same frame and photo on the stair wall; thin dust line along the top edge; faces and any hands in the photos look physically real; no text or logos.

The prompts above are the ones used for the published images. The routine first wrote shorter versions; both were redone once (main shot: frames too large and too new; close-up: boy on a beach did not match the boy on the wall, dust too thick).
