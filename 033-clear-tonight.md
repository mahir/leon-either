# No. 033 Clear Tonight

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
Documentary night photograph on the flat gravel roof of a tall building in the centre of a large city. In the foreground, two bare adult hands hold up a round white card star wheel about 25 centimetres across: a white upper disc with one oval window cut in it, over a lower disc that shows through the window as plain white card with exactly three small black dots, and nothing else in the window. The card is lit softly by the city's glow. Behind it, out of focus, lit office towers and a hazy sky that glows a flat orange grey with no stars at all. A low brick parapet runs along the bottom of the frame. Crisp contemporary colour photograph, handheld, slight grain from low light, natural and unstaged. Square format, 1:1. No faces, no logos, no brand names, no text or numbers on the card, no moon, no stars in the sky.
```
