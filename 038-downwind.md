# No. 038 Downwind

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
A colour contemporary documentary photograph of the ridge of a grey slate roof on a small town house, on an overcast autumn afternoon. On the ridge stands a black iron weathervane on a vertical rod, with four short fixed crossarms ending in the letters N, E, S and W. Above the crossarms the vane's long horizontal arrow points to the right of the frame. The arrow is built backwards: the broad flat tail fin is at the right end, and a pointed arrowhead is bolted onto the outer tip of that tail fin, so the point and the fin are at the same right hand end; the left end is a plain thin bare rod. A brick chimney stands just to the right of the weathervane, and pale grey smoke from its pot drifts clearly to the right, the same direction the arrow points. A few dry leaves blow to the right across the sky. Shot from a neighbouring upper window, eye level with the vane, the whole weathervane and chimney in frame with grey sky around them. Soft flat daylight, muted colours. Square format, 1:1. No people, no birds, no logos, no brand names, no other text besides N, E, S and W.
```

The frame shows the broad flat tail fin of the vane, cut from black sheet iron, and a separate pointed iron arrowhead fixed onto the outer tip of that fin with two shiny new steel bolts and nuts, clearly added later: the old iron is weathered with small rust spots, the bolts are bright. The point faces the right of the frame. Three dry brown leaves blur past from left to right, in the direction the point faces. Shot from slightly below, crisp focus on the bolts. Square format, 1:1.
