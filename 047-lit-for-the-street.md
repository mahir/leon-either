# No. 047 Lit for the Street

## Image 1 (main shot)

```
A crisp contemporary colour photograph taken from the pavement across a quiet city street on a cold, clear winter evening, just after dusk, deep blue sky. Facing the camera is a plain four storey red brick apartment building with a row of four sash windows on each floor. Every window is dark except exactly one: the second window from the left on the second floor, which glows warm yellow. Through that one window you can see only a bare pale wall and the soft glow of a small lamp standing low by the glass; no curtains, no furniture, no people. Two streetlamps light the pavement in front of the building with cool white light; a few parked cars are dark shapes at the kerb. Shot at eye level, slightly upward, the building filling most of the frame, documentary and still, not glossy. Square format, 1:1. No people, no logos, no brand names, no signs, no text anywhere.
```

## Image 2

```
A quiet documentary colour photograph taken at night inside a bare, unfurnished room, from low down behind and to the side of a small table lamp. The lamp has a cream fabric drum shade and a plain ceramic base, and it stands directly on old wooden floorboards, right up against the lower pane of a tall sash window with no curtains. Its white cord runs along the floor to a round white plug-in mechanical timer with a ring of small push-down pins, plugged into a white wall socket on the skirting board just below the window. The lamp lights the window glass, the window frame and a strip of skirting board; the rest of the room falls into darkness, walls bare. Through the glass, the dark street outside, two lit streetlamps and the dark brick front of the building opposite. Square format, 1:1. No people, no furniture other than the lamp, no logos, no brand names, no text anywhere.
```

Checks: exactly one lit window, on the second floor, in image 1; the lamp on the floor against the glass and the timer in the socket in image 2; no people in either. Also relative scale of lamp, socket, timer and window.
